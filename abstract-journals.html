Abstract Submission

Participants wishing to present a talk or poster at ICWANA2027 are invited to follow the author guidelines below:

  1. Download the official LaTeX Template for the talk abstract.
  2. Complete your talk title, author list, affiliations, presenting author's email, supporting agencies, and references.
  3. Submit both the .tex and compiled .pdf files via our online submission form or email to iwana2022bangkok@gmail.com.
